# Replication-code audit

This file records the final independent checks for the corrected replication
package. The legacy files under `original/codes/` and
`original/revision_figure7/` were retained as references. The minimally
corrected Figure 2 driver under `original/revision_figure2/` is updated together
with the shared runtime and editor-facing scripts under `R/` and `scripts/`.

## Necessary corrections

- Symmetric WPCA selects the eigenvectors associated with the largest
  algebraic eigenvalues of the symmetrized matrix, rather than singular vectors
  ordered by eigenvalue magnitude.
- The simulation DGP uses \(\Phi=0.9I_r\) and
  \(L=1.2U_SD_S\).
- Reported projector errors are unnormalised Frobenius norms.
- Figure 2 uses a symmetric lag-one weight, the corrected DGP, and checks that
  the population weighted signal eigenvalues are positive.
- Manuscript Figure 3 (generated by `scripts/Figure7.R`) uses ten expanding
  windows for both FRED-MD and FRED-QD.
- Loading and factor inference use rotation-compatible whitening matrices.
- Lag-matrix construction uses explicit adjacent-index assignment, including
  the \(T=2\) edge case.

## Final validation

- All 26 R files parse.
- An indefinite-matrix unit test confirms that symmetric WPCA selects the
  largest algebraic eigenvector and not the largest-magnitude singular mode.
- The loading/factor whitening checks have maximum residual below
  \(1.1\times10^{-14}\).
- All 11 unique manuscript PNGs are byte-identical to their files in
  `outputs/figs/`.
- Machine extraction from the TeX tables agrees exactly with all 48
  off-diagonal simulation cells, 48 diagonal simulation cells, 54 FRED cells,
  and 32 CV cells in the CSV files.
- Displayed minima and ties are correctly bolded.

## Claim boundaries

- AdaWPCA is the unique displayed minimum in all 16 off-diagonal U/V cells.
- Under diagonal covariance, HeteroPCA is the unique U minimum in all eight
  settings; AdaWPCA is the unique V minimum in all eight settings.
- In the FRED table, AdaWPCA is uniquely smallest in 16 of 18 cells, tied in
  one cell, and larger by 0.003 in the FRED-QD rank-one, \(q_{\rm tr}=0.9\)
  cell. Reconstruction error generally, but not uniformly, decreases with
  \(q_{\rm tr}\).
- For CV, the Non-Bottom-3 rate exceeds one half in 13 of 16 settings. The
  off-diagonal setting performs better than the diagonal setting in this run;
  no statement should claim the opposite.
- The inference plots support a finite-sample normal approximation, not exact
  finite-sample centering or unit variance.
